# Daniel Pearson — research website

Static personal research website: https://danguy5991.github.io/

The September 2026 refresh centres on proposed doctoral research about Indigenous ways of knowing, AI decisions and community accountability. Research plans are distinguished from completed work and funding outcomes.

## Pages

- `index.html`: research profile, selected writing, teaching cases and background.
- `plain.html`: the same profile in a simpler reading presentation.
- `cv.html`: academic CV with publication-status and scheduled-conferral qualifications.
- `research.css` and `research.js`: responsive styles and optional navigation enhancement.
- `sensemaking.html`: existing background page, retaining its original `style.css` and supporting assets.

No build step is required. Serve the repository with any static web server. Essential content works without JavaScript. The CV supports browser printing.

When changing the main profile, keep `plain.html` in sync. Maintain distinctions among proposed research, completed work, accepted publications, preprints and manuscripts under review. Do not infer that a scholarship application is an award or that planned doctoral relationships and approvals are already established.
