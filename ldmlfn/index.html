Before you share: how your information will be used

Draft for facilitator review — confirm before participant use.

  • Who can see your answers? You can review your own draft summary. Facilitators may see attendance details. Full answers stay in your account unless you choose to share or export them.
  • Where is information stored? On the device/browser you use for this survey (encrypted with your account password). This is a GitHub Pages site with no separate survey database by default.
  • Email and password: Used so you can return to your own draft. Entering an email identifies an account; it is not government ID verification.
  • AI follow-ups: Optional clarifying questions may be generated after an answer. If a remote AI service is enabled by facilitators, only what is needed for that follow-up would be sent — this must be confirmed before launch.
  • Correct or delete: You should be able to review and correct your summary before sharing. Ask a facilitator if you need a password reset or account answers cleared.
