How this works

Choose a product module (SharePoint, Teams, Excel, and more). You answer a simple question about challenges or understanding. The guide then crafts a follow-up from what you said — still about that product, while listening for culture, relationships, and Indigenous understanding of the situation.

Your answers stay in your profile on this browser until you export them. Other profiles cannot open your survey.
